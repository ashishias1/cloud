Our Complete Solutions

We solve infrastructure problems for every scenario — cloud, physical, hybrid, or starting from zero.

1. Cloud Cost Optimization

Steady workloads → co-location. Save 40-60% on cloud bills.

56% avg savings

The Problem: You're paying premium prices for steady workloads (databases, core apps) that don't need cloud elasticity. 60% of cloud bills are wasted.

Our Solution: We analyze your cloud usage, identify steady vs elastic workloads, move steady workloads to co-location, and set up direct connect.

Result: ₹4 Lakh/month → ₹1.75 Lakh/month. Save ₹2.25 Lakh/month (56% reduction).

2. Physical Server Migration

Lift & shift to Mumbai DCs. Zero downtime, 99.99% uptime.

4-6 hour window

The Problem: Office server rooms have power fluctuations, inadequate cooling, security risks, and maintenance overload.

Our Solution: We assess, prepare, relocate, validate, and optimize your servers to enterprise data centers with zero downtime.

Result: Zero unplanned downtime. 99.99% uptime. 66% power cost reduction.

3. Hybrid Cloud + Co-location

Best of both — steady in co-location, elastic in cloud.

2-3ms latency

The Problem: Cloud-only is expensive for steady workloads. On-premise-only can't scale. You need both.

Our Solution: We design a hybrid architecture with co-location for steady workloads, cloud for elastic workloads, and direct connect for seamless communication.

Result: 56% cost savings while keeping cloud scalability and performance.

4. Turnkey Setup (From Zero)

Design, procure, deploy, manage — everything included.

₹3,000/month

The Problem: No IT infrastructure, no expertise, no office space. You need professional IT but don't know where to start.

Our Solution: We discover your needs, design the architecture, procure hardware at volume discounts, deploy in our data center, and manage everything 24/7.

Result: Professional IT from day one. No capital expense. Start small, scale as you grow.

5. Security & Compliance

ISO 27001, SOC2, biometric access, 24/7 surveillance.

Audit-ready

The Problem: Office server rooms have basic security. Cloud security is logical only. Compliance audits often fail.

Our Solution: We provide physical security (biometric, CCTV), logical security (firewall, encryption), and compliance frameworks (ISO 27001, SOC2, GDPR).

Result: Full compliance, 100% data security, passed audits in first attempt.

6. Resource Optimization

Right-size, consolidate, automate — reduce waste.

70% utilization

The Problem: Most servers run at 20-30% utilization. 70-80% waste. Over-provisioned cloud instances. Zombie resources.

Our Solution: We discover actual usage, right-size resources, consolidate workloads, implement auto-scaling, and continuously monitor.

Result: Utilization from 20% to 70%. 49% cost reduction (₹18 Lakh → ₹9.2 Lakh).
